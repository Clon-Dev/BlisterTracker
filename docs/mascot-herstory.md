# Women's history milestones ("Herstory")

Generated by `node scripts/facts-index.ts` from [src/mascot/herstory.ts](../src/mascot/herstory.ts). 24 milestones Nyxi tells, each with the page it comes from (milestones with a date are told "On this day" first). Only what the source states: no quotes, no opinions, nothing contested. German texts are in herstory.ts.

- **1754** `erxleben-doctorate`: In 1754, Dorothea Erxleben received a doctorate in medicine from the University of Halle – the first German woman doctor with a doctorate. ([Deutsches Ärzteblatt](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/archiv/137624/Dorothea-Christiane-Erxleben-Doktor-der-Arzeneygelahrtheit))
- **1900 (02-28)** `baden-university`: On 28 February 1900, Baden became the first German state to officially admit women to university. Four women enrolled in Heidelberg that summer. ([Universität Heidelberg](https://www.uni-heidelberg.de/studium/journal/2014/12/studentinnen.html))
- **1903** `curie-nobel`: In 1903, Marie Curie became the first woman to win a Nobel Prize – the Nobel Prize in Physics, together with her husband Pierre. ([American Institute of Physics](https://history.aip.org/exhibits/curie/brief/04_honors/honors_1.html))
- **1911 (03-19)** `iwd-first`: On 19 March 1911, International Women's Day was marked for the first time – in Austria, Denmark, Germany and Switzerland, with over a million people at rallies. ([UN WomenWatch](https://www.un.org/womenwatch/feature/iwd/history.html))
- **1918 (11-12)** `suffrage-de`: On 12 November 1918, Germany decided on universal suffrage for men and women aged 20 and over. Women could vote for the first time. ([bpb](https://www.bpb.de/kurz-knapp/taegliche-dosis-politik/544647/frauenwahlrecht-in-deutschland/))
- **1919 (01-19)** `first-vote-de`: On 19 January 1919, women in Germany voted and stood for election for the first time: 82.3% of eligible women voted, and 37 women were elected. ([bpb](https://www.bpb.de/kurz-knapp/taegliche-dosis-politik/544647/frauenwahlrecht-in-deutschland/))
- **1919 (02-19)** `juchacz-speech`: On 19 February 1919, Marie Juchacz gave the first speech by a woman in a democratically elected German parliament. She began with “Gentlemen and ladies!” ([Deutscher Bundestag](https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/1919-02-19-juchacz-591728))
- **1919** `noether-habilitation`: In 1919, Emmy Noether was finally allowed to habilitate in Göttingen. She is one of the pioneers of modern algebra. ([Universität Göttingen](https://www.uni-goettingen.de/de/emmy+noether+(1882+bis+1935)/74993.html))
- **1938** `meitner-fission`: In 1938, physicist Lise Meitner and her nephew Otto Robert Frisch found the explanation for splitting atomic nuclei – and coined the term “nuclear fission”. ([Deutsches Museum](https://blog.deutsches-museum.de/2024/06/21/lise-meitner-und-die-spaltung-von-atomkernen))
- **1949** `selbert-basic-law`: Lawyer Elisabeth Selbert, one of only four women in the Parliamentary Council, fought for the sentence in Germany's 1949 Basic Law: “Men and women shall have equal rights.” ([bpb](https://www.bpb.de/themen/gender-diversitaet/frauenbewegung/35275/frauen-und-maenner-sind-gleichberechtigt/))
- **1958 (07-01)** `equality-act-1958`: On 1 July 1958, West Germany's Equality Act came into force: husbands lost the final say in marriage, and a wife's property no longer belonged to her husband. ([Deutscher Bundestag](https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2017/kw17-kalenderblatt-gleichberechtigungsgesetz-504286))
- **1960 (08-18)** `enovid-us`: On 18 August 1960, the first pill, Enovid, came onto the US market as a contraceptive. ([bpb](https://www.bpb.de/kurz-knapp/hintergrund-aktuell/314141/60-jahre-pille/))
- **1961 (06-01)** `anovlar-de`: On 1 June 1961, the first pill came onto the market in West Germany: Anovlar. Officially, at first, it was for menstrual complaints. ([bpb](https://www.bpb.de/kurz-knapp/hintergrund-aktuell/314141/60-jahre-pille/))
- **1963 (06-16)** `tereshkova-space`: On 16 June 1963, Valentina Tereshkova became the first woman in space. On Vostok 6, she orbited the Earth 49 times in 70.8 hours. ([ESA](https://www.esa.int/About_Us/ESOC/First_woman_in_space_visits_ESOC))
- **1965** `gdr-pill`: From 1965, the pill in the GDR was advertised as the “Wunschkindpille” – the “wanted-child pill”. ([bpb](https://www.bpb.de/kurz-knapp/hintergrund-aktuell/314141/60-jahre-pille/))
- **1975** `un-iwd`: In 1975, International Women's Year, the United Nations began celebrating International Women's Day on 8 March. ([UN WomenWatch](https://www.un.org/womenwatch/feature/iwd/history.html))
- **1977 (07-01)** `marriage-reform-1977`: From 1 July 1977, married women in West Germany no longer needed their husband's consent to take a job. ([bpb](https://www.bpb.de/kurz-knapp/hintergrund-aktuell/576893/1976-bundestag-reformiert-das-ehe-und-familienrecht/))
- **1983** `hiv-discovery`: In 1983, Françoise Barré-Sinoussi and Luc Montagnier discovered HIV at the Institut Pasteur. They received the Nobel Prize in Medicine for it in 2008. ([Institut Pasteur](https://www.pasteur.fr/en/nobel-prize-medicine-2008-awarded-professors-francoise-barre-sinoussi-and-luc-montagnier))
- **1995 (10-09)** `nuesslein-volhard-nobel`: On 9 October 1995, Christiane Nüsslein-Volhard was announced as the first German woman to receive the Nobel Prize in Medicine – for research on early embryonic development. ([Max-Planck-Gesellschaft](https://www.mpg.de/15479017/25-jahre-nobelpreis-christiane-nuesslein-volhard))
- **2006** `hpv-vaccine`: Since 2006, vaccines against the most important high-risk HPV types have been available. Harald zur Hausen won the 2008 Nobel Prize for showing that HPV causes cervical cancer. ([DKFZ](https://www.dkfz.de/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/detail/harald-zur-hausen-zum-85-geburtstag))
- **2009** `menopause-day`: In 2009, the International Menopause Society founded World Menopause Day. It's held every year on 18 October. ([International Menopause Society](https://www.imsociety.org/education/world-menopause-day/))
- **2014 (05-28)** `mh-day`: On 28 May 2014, the first Menstrual Hygiene Day took place, initiated by the organisation WASH United. ([Menstrual Hygiene Day](https://www.menstrualhygieneday.org/about))
- **2015** `morning-after-pill-de`: Since March 2015, the morning-after pill has been available in German pharmacies without a prescription. ([Deutsches Ärzteblatt](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/nachrichten/62167))
- **2018** `hpv-boys-de`: In June 2018, Germany's vaccination committee STIKO decided to recommend the HPV vaccine for boys aged 9 to 14 as well. ([DKFZ](https://www.dkfz.de/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/detail/das-wurde-auch-hoechste-zeit-hpv-impfung-fuer-jungen-empfohlen))
